# Minecraft-only scope

This repository models CC:Tweaked and Sable behavior inside a specific Minecraft 1.21.1 NeoForge modpack. References to constructions, engines, cannons, projectiles, fuzes, explosions, forces and trajectories name in-game blocks, entities, peripherals and synthetic simulation values.

The tools are not real-world engineering or weapon-design software. They do not contain material specifications, manufacturing procedures or validated real-world models. Keep reports in Minecraft blocks, ticks, mod identifiers and explicitly labeled simulation units. Do not extrapolate results into real-world construction guidance.

This scope statement supplies context for assistants and reviewers. It does not request bypassing product safeguards. Ambiguous requests should be clarified by asking for the Minecraft mod, block, entity or peripheral involved.
